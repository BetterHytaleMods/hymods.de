Zoom Goggles I–IV

Hold R to zoom in, from 1.5x up to 8x. FlexiisGoggles uses Hytale's own client zoom (the one the spyglass uses): the view narrows smoothly, the item in your hand goes away, the mouse slows down and the camera glides after it. Let go to zoom back out.

R is Hytale's “Use Ability 3”; bind that action to any key under Settings → Controls.

Night Vision Goggles I–IV

Caves and nights light up – for you alone. Torches, lava and the sun still shine brighter, so the world keeps its depth. Nobody else sees it, and mobs and plants keep using the real light.

/nightvision (or /nv) switches it on and off.

UltraGoggles

Zoom up to 16x and the strongest night vision in one late-game pair. They need lumenite – a rare ore that looks like plain stone to everyone, unless you look through Night Vision Goggles IV.

Optician's Bench

A cheap bench that grows through five tiers – from a trestle table with a grindstone to a gold-trimmed desk with a floating lumenite crystal. Every tier unlocks stronger goggles; from tier 3 the bench lights up the room, and its grindstone turns while crafting. Glass and lenses are made there too.

Crafting

Getting started

  1. Craft the Optician's Bench at the Workbench: 4 Copper Ingot, 10 Any Tree Log, 5 Any Stone.
  2. Mine Quartz Ore in the stone of zones 1–3; quartz goes into nearly everything. At the bench, sand and quartz make glass, glass and more quartz make lenses, lenses make goggles.
  3. Upgrade the bench for stronger goggles: iron, quartz and light leather for tier 2, then thorium, gold and crystal shards, then adamantite, void crystals and voidhearts – and more quartz every time.
  4. Night Vision Goggles IV show lumenite, which looks like plain stone to everyone else. Three of it go into the UltraGoggles at bench tier 5.
  5. At the Armorer's Workbench, goggles and a vanilla helmet of their tier make a helm.

Helms

Mount your goggles on a helmet at the Armorer's Workbench and wear both: Scout Helms (zoom), Nightwatch Helms (night vision) and the Overseer Helm. Helms count like the goggles mounted on them and keep the helmet's armor values: copper for I, iron for II, thorium or cobalt for III, adamantite for IV and the UltraGoggles.

  • Copper Scout Helm Zoom Goggles I + Copper Helm Armorer's Workbench, tier 1
  • Iron Scout Helm Zoom Goggles II + Iron Helm Armorer's Workbench, tier 1
  • Thorium Scout Helm Zoom Goggles III + Thorium Helm Armorer's Workbench, tier 2
  • Cobalt Scout Helm Zoom Goggles III + Cobalt Helm Armorer's Workbench, tier 2
  • Adamantite Scout Helm Zoom Goggles IV + Adamantite Helm Armorer's Workbench, tier 3
  • Copper Nightwatch Helm Night Vision Goggles I + Copper Helm Armorer's Workbench, tier 1

Quartz & Lumenite

Quartz Ore

A new crystal ore in the stone of zones 1–3. Quartz goes into nearly everything.

  • Zones 1–3
  • Cave stone
  • y 30–105

Lumenite Ore

A rare crystal deep in the stone that looks like plain stone to everyone. Only Night Vision Goggles IV and UltraGoggles (also mounted on a helm) show it, while their night vision is on.

  • Rare
  • Zones 1–3
  • y 20–75
  • 3 per UltraGoggles

Both ores only appear in newly generated chunks.

Settings

mods/de.hymods_FlexiisGoggles/config.json, created on the first start. /goggles reload applies changes without a restart.

Settings in config.json
SettingMeaning
requireGogglestrue: zoom and night vision only while wearing goggles. false: everyone gets both, up to 16x and brightness 6
zoom.enabled, nightVision.enabledSwitch a feature off for everyone
goggles.<name>.zoom, goggles.<name>.nightVisionHow strong each pair of goggles is on this server; can only be lowered
zoom.magnification, nightVision.levelWhat players get without goggles (requireGoggles: false) until they pick their own
zoom.sensitivityMouse sensitivity at 4x, as a share of the normal one; other strengths scale it
zoom.inLerp, zoom.outLerpHow fast the view zooms in and out (0.01 slow to 1 instant)
zoom.glideDefault glide while zoomed, 0 (off) to 1.8

Each player's own choices are in players.json next to it. Switching night vision on or changing its brightness makes that player's client rebuild the world it has loaded, which can show as a short flicker.

Notes

  • Quartz and lumenite ore only appear in chunks generated after the mod was installed; in an existing world, explore new land.
  • Removing the mod later leaves its blocks (ores, benches) in the world as unknown blocks.
  • R keeps its usual job while you hold a block (rotating it), while the rune in ability slot 3 fits your weapon (casting it), and in game modes or with plugins that use R themselves.
